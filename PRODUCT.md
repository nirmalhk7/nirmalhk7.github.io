Keep the personal beach and trees photography, red accent #c43b31, warm paper #f5f2eb, charcoal #202321, Outfit, and Plex Mono. Code-first refinement of the existing identity, approved by the user.

## Evidence on Hand
Experience and education are recorded in content/yml. Project facts and the Whirlwind workflow are recorded in content/projects. Article dates and descriptions come from content/blog. Claims and diagrams must stay grounded in those sources.

## Product Principles

- Make project evidence easy to inspect.
- Keep content visible without animation or JavaScript.
- Preserve accurate qualifiers on achievements.
